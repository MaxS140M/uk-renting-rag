# Error analysis: `hybrid_rerank_bge`

A catalogue of the failures on the dev split, generated by `scripts/evaluation/error_analysis.py`. Each failure's likely cause is assigned **automatically** from where the correct passage ranked; the examples were not individually diagnosed by hand. The summary below is written by hand.

## Summary

<!-- summary:start (hand-written; kept when this file is regenerated) -->
- **Main weakness: false refusals.** The system refused 15 of 99 answerable dev questions
  (15%) that the guidance does answer, and correctly refused all 11 unanswerable ones. It errs
  towards refusing, which is the safer failure for legal information but still a failure.
- **Retrieval explains about a third of the failures.** In 25 of the 65 failures the passage
  holding the gold evidence was not among the 5 given to the model (16 not in the top 10, 9
  ranked 6 to 10). Part of this is the test set: each question has a single gold passage,
  but the guidance often repeats a rule in several places, so some "misses" retrieved a
  different passage that also answers the question.
- **The other 40 had the gold passage but still fell short of a fully correct, fully
  supported answer.** That count is strict (one unsupported claim is enough), so it mixes
  real misreadings with minor overstatements and some judge strictness.

**Patterns confirmed when reviewing the 16 answers where my labels and the judge disagreed**
([details](judge/agreement.md)). These come from that review of 27 sampled answers, not from
diagnosing every failure:

- **Rules from the wrong sector.** A private renter was told their landlord must act within
  24 hours, a rule from Awaab's Law, which covers social housing (q-116).
- **Hedged guidance stated as certain.** "You may be in priority need" became "you are in
  priority need" (q-080).
- **Rules stretched beyond the passage.** A rule about asking for rent in advance was
  presented as making a £300 admin fee illegal (q-111); the conclusion happens to be true
  under the Tenant Fees Act, but not from the passages given.
- **Conclusions about the user's situation that the passages do not support**, such as "your
  landlord is now overdue" when the user never said the amount had been agreed (q-006, q-106).
- **Missing key facts the guidance contains**, with the answer saying it could not find them:
  compensation of up to 3 times the deposit (q-082); that landlords cannot charge for pet
  insurance (q-103).
- **The judge is sometimes too strict:** in 4 of the 14 disputed faithfulness calls the
  answer was fine (for example, describing Citizens Advice as "free advice").

Not done: diagnosing each failure by hand, and counting how often the judge or the reference
answer, rather than the system, was wrong.
